I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at Purdue University. Previously, I completed both my B.S. (December 2024) and M.S. (May 2026) in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, advised by [Prof. Ali Anwar](https://chalianwar.github.io). My research interests broadly span machine learning and intelligent systems, particularly how to make these systems learn continuously. I'm also especially interested in how these ideas extend towards robotics and the physical world.
